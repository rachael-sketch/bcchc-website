Primary care services

Family medicine for every stage of life at our clinics in Black Canyon City and Mayer.

Everyone

  • Treatment of acute illnesses, such as sore throat or flu, and injuries
  • Management of chronic conditions, including high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, allergies, heart disease and asthma
  • Annual exams and physicals
  • Care for several related health conditions at once
  • Immunizations
  • Weight management
  • Help quitting smoking

Women

  • Well-woman exams
  • Gynecological care
  • Family planning
  • Hormone management

Children

  • Well-child checkups
  • Vaccines
  • Sick visits for sore throat, flu and other illnesses

Ages 65+

  • Medicare Annual Wellness Visits
  • Help enrolling in Medicare
  • Age-specific screenings

Behavioral health

  • Screening for depression and anxiety during primary care visits
  • Referrals to counseling and substance use treatment in the community

Help getting care

  • Translation services
  • Insurance and eligibility help
  • Help enrolling in AHCCCS and other public programs
  • Referrals to partner providers for dental and prenatal care, and to specialists
  • Our Sliding Fee Discount Program

More about our care

Primary care that follows you over time

A provider who knows you

Seeing the same care team visit after visit means fewer repeated questions and care that fits your whole history, not just today's symptom.

Ongoing care for chronic conditions

For diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, asthma and heart disease, we track your numbers, adjust medications and plan regular follow-ups so problems are caught early.

Prevention at every age

Well-child checkups and vaccines, well-woman exams, adult physicals and Medicare Annual Wellness Visits keep screenings up to date.

Help with the whole picture

Many patients manage more than one condition. We look at how they affect each other and keep your medications and care plan in one place.

Mental health matters too

We screen for depression and anxiety as part of primary care and connect you with counseling in the community when you need more support.

Close to home

Our clinics serve Black Canyon City, Mayer and nearby rural communities, so you don't have to drive to Phoenix or Prescott for everyday care.

Not sure if we can help?

Call either clinic and ask. If we don't offer a service, we'll help you find a provider who does.
