Why work with us

Meaningful work, close to home

Our providers and staff get to know their patients and make a real difference in small communities.

Mission-driven care

Join a nonprofit community health center that has cared for rural central Arizona since 1999, for patients with or without insurance.

Loan repayment eligibility

We are a National Health Service Corps-approved site, so eligible clinicians may qualify for NHSC student loan repayment.

A growing organization

We served 3,100 patients in 2025, up 14% from 2024, and are opening a third clinic in Camp Verde.

Weekday schedules

Our clinics are open Monday through Friday, with no weekend clinic hours.
