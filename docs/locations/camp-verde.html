Primary care is coming to the Verde Valley

Black Canyon Community Health Center is opening its third clinic on Main Street in Camp Verde. We'll offer the same family medicine and preventive care available at our other clinics.

Hours and online booking will be posted on this page when the clinic opens. Questions? Call (928) 222-2218. Until then, Camp Verde residents are welcome at either of our current clinics.
