Before your visit

  • Bring a photo ID and your insurance card, if you have one.
  • Bring a list of your medications, or the bottles themselves.
  • No insurance? Ask about our sliding fee discount when you check in.
  • Please arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit.

More options in the portal

Current patients can request appointments, message their care team and see upcoming visits in the patient portal.

For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
