Sliding Fee Discount Program

If your household income is at or below 200% of the federal poverty guidelines, you pay a reduced, flat fee for your visit.

How it works

Our goal is to remove cost as a reason to skip care, especially for patients and families at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines. The discount is based only on household size and income. It's available to everyone who qualifies, whether or not you have insurance.

  1. Pick up a sliding fee application at the front desk of either clinic.
  2. Bring it back with proof of household income, such as recent pay stubs, a tax return or a benefits letter.
  3. We'll tell you your discount level. You pay that fee at each visit.

Insured patients who qualify never pay more than the sliding fee amount for their visit. Our staff can also help you see if you qualify for AHCCCS.
